## My presentation

For my class presentation, I chose to talk about **how deepfakes work**. Deepfakes are fake videos, images or voices made by artificial intelligence, and they look very real. I picked this topic because it combines AI with a real problem in our daily lives, and I want to understand how this technology works and how we can recognize it.
