## My presentation topic

My topic is **how deepfakes work**. Deepfakes are fake videos, images or voices made by artificial intelligence, and they can look very real. I chose this topic because it connects AI with a real problem in our daily lives.

In my presentation, I will explain:

1. What a deepfake is and where we can see it.
2. How AI models learn to create fake faces and voices.
3. How people and tools can detect deepfakes.
4. Why this technology matters for all of us.
